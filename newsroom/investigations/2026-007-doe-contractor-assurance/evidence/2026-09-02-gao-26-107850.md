# E1 — GAO-26-107850, Contractor Assurance Systems

- URL: https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-107850.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-02
- Filename: `GAO-26-107850.pdf` (official accessible HTML text also retrieved at https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-107850/index.html)
- Locator: Highlights, “What GAO Found”; Letter, pp. 1–4; “CAS Key Elements and Requirements.”
- Captured excerpt: GAO reports that selected DOE field offices evaluated contractor assurance systems even though DOE and EM had not defined effectiveness or specified evaluation criteria. GAO says Hanford, Idaho, and Los Alamos field-office determinations lacked a defined effectiveness standard and that all three reported poor CAS performance or recurring issues. The report also states that EM selected three contracts based in part on contract value and purpose and reviewed contracts, CAS descriptions, assessments, oversight schedules, and performance-evaluation documents.
- Why it matters: Establishes the policy-to-practice test and identifies the contract and performance records needed for an original finding.
