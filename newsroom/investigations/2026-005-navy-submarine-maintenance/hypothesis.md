# Hypothesis

The Navy's FY2026 maintenance funding and shipyard-capacity response may not yet demonstrate recovery of the attack-submarine maintenance backlog against its stated 20% maintenance benchmark.

The January 2026 CRS report supplies a new class-level test: the number of SSNs in depot maintenance or awaiting depot maintenance increased from 11 boats, or 21% of the force, in FY2012 to 16 boats, or 33%, in FY2023, and remained 16 boats, or 34%, in FY2024. The Navy response described in the same record is increased shipyard staffing plus the multibillion-dollar Shipyard Infrastructure Optimization Program. The next record must establish whether that response changed the measure after FY2024; the comparison is not yet a finding.
