# E4 — GAO-19-229, attack-submarine maintenance delays

- URL: https://files.gao.gov/assets/gao-19-229.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-08-30
- Locator: PDF pp. 12–14.
- Captured excerpt: GAO reported that the Navy had not completed a comprehensive business-case analysis for allocating attack-submarine maintenance between public and private shipyards. Without a complete accounting of costs, benefits, and risks, GAO said the Navy could not weigh private-yard costs against reduced idle time and added operational availability.
- Why it matters: This historical oversight record identifies the cost-and-capacity decision gap that later Navy execution records may show as resolved or persistent.
