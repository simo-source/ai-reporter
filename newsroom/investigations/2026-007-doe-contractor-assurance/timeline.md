# Timeline

- 1990 — DOE contractor management and oversight entered GAO's High Risk List, according to GAO-26-107850.
- 2022 — The Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board reported that DOE used CAS information without sufficient documented justification that it was reliable and effective, according to GAO-26-107850.
- 2024 — DOE's Office of Enterprise Assessments reported that contractors inadequately managed 25 percent of issues in a representative sample of about 4,000 issues across nine sites, according to GAO-26-107850.
- 2026-08-26 — GAO published GAO-26-107850 on DOE's evaluation of selected contractor assurance systems.
- 2026-09-02 — DOE's official oversight-policy page was paired with GAO-26-107850; the investigation opened to test policy requirements against contract-level fee and performance records.
