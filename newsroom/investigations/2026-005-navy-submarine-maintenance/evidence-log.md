## E1 — Congressional Research Service, Navy Virginia-Class Submarine Program

- URL: https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/RL/PDF/RL32418/RL32418.295.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-08-30
- Locator: January 26, 2026 report, PDF pp. 7–8, “SSN Maintenance Backlog,” Table 1, and following paragraph.
- What it establishes: FY2024 had 16 SSNs in depot maintenance or awaiting depot maintenance, 34% of the 47-boat force, with 31 operationally ready; the report gives a 20% maintenance benchmark, attributes the increase primarily to workers/facility constraints, and describes increased workers plus SIOP as the response.

## Interpretation boundary

E1 creates a measurable comparison with the Navy's stated benchmark, but no source captured so far reports a post-FY2024 class-level outcome. Resource days, combat-surge readiness, procurement cadence, and inactive-idle days are not interchangeable measures.
