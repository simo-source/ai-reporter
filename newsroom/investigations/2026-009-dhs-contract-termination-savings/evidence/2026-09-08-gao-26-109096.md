# E1 — GAO-26-109096, DHS 2025 Contract Terminations

- URL: https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-109096.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-08
- Locator: PDF p. 2, “Key Takeaways”; PDF p. 1, opening paragraph.
- Captured excerpt: GAO reports that DHS fully or partially terminated 438 contracts from January 20 through September 30, 2025. More than $1.6 billion had been obligated on those contracts before termination, and DHS later deobligated a net total of more than $92 million. GAO says DHS publicly reported that the terminations could potentially avoid $10.5 billion, but that figure is based on the maximum amount DHS could have obligated and does not establish what would actually have been obligated absent termination.
- Why it matters: Establishes the first population, date range, obligation field, and net-deobligation measure to reconcile against DHS's financial report and termination records.
