## Hypothesis

DOD's 90-percent installation-maintenance funding goal may not be a reliable proxy for reducing the facilities backlog because service budget requests, funded requirements, and executed work may measure different populations or time periods.

## What would be new

The new finding would reconcile GAO's funding and backlog measures with service budget-justification and execution records, showing whether the 90-percent goal tracks actual backlog reduction or leaves a measurable gap.

## Primary documents to fetch first

1. GAO-26-107255: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-107255
2. The DOD and military-service installation-maintenance budget justifications and facility-condition records cited by GAO.
3. Recent DOD sustainment funding and execution records that define the funded-needs denominator and backlog calculation.

## Human stake

Installation conditions affect service-member safety, mission readiness, and billions of dollars in public maintenance obligations.

## Why it might be wrong

The apparent gap may result from different accounting periods or facility populations rather than a mismatch in DOD's funding goal, and the underlying service records may show that funded work is reducing the backlog.
