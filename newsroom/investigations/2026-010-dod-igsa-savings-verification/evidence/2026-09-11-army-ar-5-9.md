- Excerpt: The regulation assigns the Army's installation oversight office responsibility for processing reports twice a year to Army senior leaders, the Office of the Secretary of Defense, and Congress, and requires quarterly reporting to the oversight office. An IGSA submission must include a cost-benefit analysis demonstrating savings against other courses of action; if approved, the originating command receives an approval letter and must send a copy of the completed agreement to the oversight office within 90 days. Installations must monitor and manage each IGSA, retain all IGSA documentation for at least six years, and appoint separate technical representatives and managers.
- Boundary: The regulation describes required process and records. It does not show that the Army's public-facing reports contain realized savings, the statutory 25-percent installation amount, or the required sustainment, restoration, and modernization uses.
