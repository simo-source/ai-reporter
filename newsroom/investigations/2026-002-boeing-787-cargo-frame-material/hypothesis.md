# Hypothesis

The FAA's proposed inspection for certain Boeing 787-8 large forward cargo-door split frames may cover a broader type-and-batch population than the four U.S.-registered airplanes used in the agency's public cost estimate.

The first comparison is now supported as a scope distinction: the incorporated bulletin lists nine 787-8 line numbers in its effectivity, while the proposal's cost section says its estimate affects four airplanes of U.S. registry. That distinction is not, by itself, evidence of undercounting or an error; the next test is to determine whether the four U.S.-registry aircraft can be identified among the nine effectivity line numbers and whether the batch list maps to the same population.
