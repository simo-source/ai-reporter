- Captured excerpt: The Army Corps of Engineers says its Civil Works maintenance workflow uses the Facilities and Equipment Maintenance system to record work orders from identification through approval, planning, procurement, scheduling, execution, inspection, and closeout. The guidance requires work orders to capture statuses, estimated and actual labor/material/service costs, dates, failure reporting, and reasons for non-completion; it defines a `WFUNDS` status for valid work waiting for funding and a backlog as valid work not currently in progress, complete, closed, or awaiting approval.
- Boundary: This is an official work-order and backlog data standard for the USACE Civil Works program, not evidence about Army regular-component FSRM execution or DOD’s Facilities Sustainment Model. It identifies the fields a comparable execution record should expose but supplies no service-wide obligation, closure, or condition series.
