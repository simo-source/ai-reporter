# Timeline

- FY2020 — DOD reported $137 billion in deferred maintenance, according to GAO-26-107255.
- FY2021–FY2025 — GAO analyzed service facility-sustainment budgets, requests, and obligations against the Facilities Sustainment Model.
- FY2025 — DOD reported an estimated $285 billion deferred-maintenance requirement, according to GAO-26-107255.
- August 21, 2026 — GAO published GAO-26-107255.
- FY2026 request — DOD's overview requests $27.3 billion for FSRM and describes sustainment at an 85-percent minimum and 87-percent average.
- September 3, 2026 — Investigation opened after comparing GAO's historical execution analysis with DOD's FY2026 request framing.
- September 4, 2026 — Army, Navy, and Marine Corps Reserve FY2026 service justification books were compared. They report 91 percent, 85 percent (conditional on reconciliation), and 53 percent of modeled sustainment requirements respectively; no same-population execution or condition outcome was found.
