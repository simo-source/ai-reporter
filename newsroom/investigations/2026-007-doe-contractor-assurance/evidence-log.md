# Evidence log

## E1 — GAO-26-107850, Contractor Assurance Systems

- URL: https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-107850.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-02
- Locator: Highlights, “What GAO Found”; Letter, pp. 1–4; “CAS Key Elements and Requirements.”
- Captured excerpt: GAO reports that selected DOE field offices evaluated contractor assurance systems even though DOE and EM had not defined effectiveness or specified evaluation criteria. GAO says Hanford, Idaho, and Los Alamos field-office determinations lacked a defined effectiveness standard and that all three reported poor CAS performance or recurring issues. The report states that EM selected three contracts based in part on contract value and purpose and reviewed contracts, CAS descriptions, assessments, oversight schedules, and performance-evaluation documents.
- Why it matters: Identifies the policy-to-practice comparison and the contract records needed for an original finding.

## E2 — DOE Department of Energy Oversight Policy

- URL: https://www.energy.gov/ehss/department-energy-oversight-policy
- Retrieved: 2026-09-02
- Locator: “Department of Energy Oversight Policy,” opening policy description.
- Captured excerpt: DOE says DOE Policy 226.2 establishes expectations for federal oversight and contractor assurance systems, while DOE Order 226.1B requires contractors to implement effective CASs that provide assurance that work is conducted safely and securely, risks are managed, and internal controls function efficiently. DOE says CAS information supports a risk-based, tailored approach to oversight across safety, security, cybersecurity, environment, quality, and business and finance.
- Why it matters: Supplies the independent policy baseline against which selected contract clauses, evaluation criteria, and fee decisions can be compared.
