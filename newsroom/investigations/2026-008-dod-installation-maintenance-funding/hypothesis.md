# Hypothesis

DOD's 90-percent installation-maintenance funding goal may not be a reliable proxy for reducing the facilities backlog because service budget requests, funded requirements, and executed work may measure different populations or time periods.

GAO's August 2026 report provides a recent comparison point: the department's services generally requested and obligated about 80 percent of the Facilities Sustainment Model recommendation during fiscal years 2021 through 2025, while reported deferred maintenance increased from $137 billion in FY2020 to an estimated $285 billion in FY2025. DOD's FY2026 budget overview presents a different forward-looking frame—$27.3 billion for Facilities Sustainment, Restoration, and Modernization and sustainment at an 85-percent minimum and 87-percent average—without, in the overview itself, joining those figures to service-level execution or backlog change.

This is a testable document-comparison hypothesis, not a finding that DOD misused funds, caused unsafe conditions, or failed to meet a legal obligation.
