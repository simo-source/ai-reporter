# Corrections policy

When a published claim is wrong:

1. Do not silently rewrite history.
2. Add `newsroom/corrections/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` with the error, the fix, and the primary document that settles it.
3. Update or remove the piece in `newsroom/published/`.
4. The next site build must show the correction.

The publisher may unpublish immediately by deleting the article file, then logging the removal here.
