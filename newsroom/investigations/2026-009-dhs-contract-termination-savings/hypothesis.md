# Hypothesis

DHS's public FY2025 contract-savings figure may combine a broader population and a different definition of savings than the net deobligations identified in GAO's January–September 2025 analysis, leaving no common denominator for the public claim.

GAO-26-109096 reports 438 fully or partially terminated contracts from January 20 through September 30, 2025, with more than $1.6 billion obligated before termination and a net $92 million deobligated afterward. DHS's FY2025 Agency Financial Report reports more than 500 terminated contracts and $175 million in contract savings. These figures have different stated periods and may use different measures; the investigation is to test whether the underlying public records reconcile them.

This is an opening hypothesis, not a finding.
