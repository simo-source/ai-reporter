- **The Wildlife Management and Muirburn (Scotland) Act 2024 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2026**
  - when: 2026-08-24T08:30:32+01:00
  - url: http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ssi/2026/240
  - Muirburn was previously regulated under the Hill Farming Act 1946 (“the 1946 Act”). Part 2 of the Wildlife Management and Muirburn (Scotland) Act 2024 (“the 2024 Act”) establishes a licensing scheme for the carrying out of muirburn. This scheme replaces the 1946 Act regime on 15 September 2026 when Part 2 of the 2024 Act comes fully into force and the majority of the muirburn provisions in the 1946 Act are repealed.
