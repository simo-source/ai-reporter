## Hypothesis

The federal uranium supply effort may present separate national-security inventory and civilian-demand assumptions without a documented bridge between them, making the program's cost and economic analysis less decision-useful than its inventory outlook.

## What would be new

The new finding would connect the GAO report's cost-reporting critique to NNSA and DOE procurement, inventory, or budget documents and identify a specific assumption that changes the reported economics.

## Primary documents to fetch first

1. GAO-26-107385: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-107385

## Human stake

## Why it might be wrong
