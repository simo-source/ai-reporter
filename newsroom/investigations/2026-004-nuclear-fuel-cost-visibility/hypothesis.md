# Hypothesis

Federal uranium-supply programs may have committed substantial public funding before DOE documented the economic analysis, strategic plan, and schedule needed to show that the awards are sized to create a sustainable domestic enrichment market.

The first two primary records establish a testable tension, not a finding: GAO reports that DOE had awarded $2.7 billion in task orders while lacking a documented economic analysis and finalized HALEU plan, while DOE's own January 2026 announcement describes the awards as a ten-year, milestone-based investment. The next document must test whether those milestones and deliverables supply the missing decision framework or leave the mismatch unresolved.
