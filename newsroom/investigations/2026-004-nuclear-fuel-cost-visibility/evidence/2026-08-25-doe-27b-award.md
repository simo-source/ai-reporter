# E2 — DOE domestic uranium-enrichment task-order announcement

- URL: https://www.energy.gov/articles/us-department-energy-awards-27-billion-restore-american-uranium-enrichment
- Retrieved: 2026-08-25T16:01Z
- Locator: DOE announcement dated January 5, 2026, sections “Enrichment Task Orders” and “Innovative Technology Funding Opportunities.”
- Captured excerpt: DOE announced $2.7 billion “over the next ten years” and said the awards would be distributed under “a strict milestone approach.” The announcement lists $900 million each for American Centrifuge Operating and General Matter to create domestic HALEU enrichment capacity, and $900 million for Orano Federal Services to expand domestic LEU capacity; it separately lists an additional $28 million award to Global Laser Enrichment.
- Why it matters: Supplies an independent agency record of the commitment, time horizon, recipients, and milestone framing. It does not disclose the economic analysis or the milestone schedule that would show whether the investment is appropriately sized.
