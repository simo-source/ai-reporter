# E5 — Navy FY2026 Operation and Maintenance justification

- URL: https://www.secnav.navy.mil/fmc/fmb/Documents/26pres/OMN_Book.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-04
- Filename: official Department of the Navy FY2026 Operation and Maintenance, Navy, Volume I justification book
- Locator: Appropriation Highlights, “Facilities Sustainment Restoration and Modernization”; Base Support, Subactivity Group BSM1, Exhibit OP-5, page 408; summary table and reconciliation explanation.
- Captured excerpt: The Navy says the FY2026 request includes $3,991.438 million discretionary and $1,686.916 million mandatory reconciliation funding, for $5,678.354 million total in BSM1 Sustainment, Restoration and Modernization. It says the mandatory amount provides facility sustainment at 85 percent of the modeled requirement, poor or failing-condition unaccompanied-housing recapitalization, and supplements the Navy’s restoration and modernization backlog. The highlights identify a $475 billion plant replacement value against which the Navy says it faces a facilities-maintenance backlog.
- Boundary: The 85-percent statement is conditional on enactment of the reconciliation bill and concerns the Navy’s modeled requirement; the book does not by itself quantify FY2026 obligations, completed work orders, or backlog change.
