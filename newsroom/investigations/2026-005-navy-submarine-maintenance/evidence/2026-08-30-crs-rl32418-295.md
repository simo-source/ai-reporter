# E1 — Congressional Research Service, Navy Virginia-Class Submarine Program

- URL: https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/RL/PDF/RL32418/RL32418.295.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-08-30T19:18:44Z
- Document date: January 26, 2026
- Locator: PDF pp. 7–8, “SSN Maintenance Backlog,” Table 1, and the paragraph immediately following the table.
- Captured excerpt: Table 1 reports 16 SSNs in depot maintenance or awaiting depot maintenance in FY2024, 34% of a 47-boat force, and 31 operationally ready. The table shows 11 boats, or 21% of the force, in the combined category in FY2012. The report says the Navy's stated industry-best-practice goal was about 20% in depot maintenance and none idle, attributes the increase primarily to insufficient workers and facility constraints at the four government-operated Naval Shipyards, and identifies supply-chain issues as an additional issue. It says the Navy increased shipyard workers and began the multibillion-dollar SIOP investment plan in 2018.
- Why it matters: This is a distinct post-May-2025 congressional record with class-level maintenance/idle data and a stated benchmark against which later Navy or budget-execution records can be tested.
