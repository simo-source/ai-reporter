# Evidence log

## E1 — GAO-26-109096, DHS 2025 Contract Terminations

- URL: https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-109096.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-08
- Locator: PDF p. 2, “Key Takeaways”; PDF p. 1, opening paragraph.
- Captured excerpt: GAO reports 438 fully or partially terminated contracts from January 20 through September 30, 2025; more than $1.6 billion had been obligated before termination, and the net deobligation afterward exceeded $92 million. GAO distinguishes this from DHS's publicly reported potential $10.5 billion cost avoidance, which was based on maximum possible obligations.
- Why it matters: Defines the first comparison population and distinguishes net deobligation from potential cost avoidance.

## E2 — DHS FY2025 Agency Financial Report, Volume 2

- Retrieved: 2026-09-08
- Locator: “Management Discussion and Analysis,” “Specific accomplishments in FY 2025,” “Cost Savings.”
- Captured excerpt: DHS states that it terminated over 350 grants and 500 contracts during FY2025, with savings of over $1 billion from grants and $175 million from contracts.
- Why it matters: Provides a second official count and savings measure, but not yet the fields needed to determine whether it reconciles with GAO's January–September analysis.
