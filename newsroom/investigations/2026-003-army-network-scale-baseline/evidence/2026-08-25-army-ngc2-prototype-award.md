# E3 — Army NGC2 prototype award

- URL: https://www.army.mil/article/287180/army_announces_next_generation_command_and_control_ngc2_prototype_award
- Retrieved: 2026-08-25
- Locator: “Army Announces Next Generation Command and Control (NGC2) Prototype Award,” paragraphs beginning “The U.S. Army will continue experimentation” and “The OTA, for $99.6 million”; later paragraphs beginning “Continuing the momentum of PCC5” and “In addition to 4th ID activities.”
- Captured excerpt: The Army describes an $99.6 million, 11-month Other Transaction Authority agreement for a prototype architecture for the 4th Infantry Division. It says the Army planned a competitive Commercial Solutions Opening for additional NGC2 prototyping, including the 25th Infantry Division and III Corps Headquarters, and that the 4th ID planned to use the prototype as its primary C2 system at PCC6 the following summer.
- Why it matters: E3 supplies an acquisition and unit-level implementation record to compare with the broader two-division-and-a-corps fielding language in E1 and the project-level prototype funding in E2.
