# E3 — DoD Instruction 4000.19, Support Agreements

- URL: https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodi/400019p.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-10
- Filename: official DoD instruction, December 16, 2020
- Locators: sections 1.1(c)–(d), 2.4(c)–(e), 3.2(a)–(d), 3.4(a)–(c), and table 1, requirements 20–22, pages 3–12.
- Captured excerpt: The instruction applies to agreements between a DoD component and a State, local, or tribal government, but says a more specific statute or policy controls if there is a conflict and that the instruction itself does not create authority to enter an agreement. Component heads must designate agreement managers, establish approval processes, and require internal controls and oversight measures.
- Additional excerpt: The instruction requires cost information to be quantifiable, measurable, and reasonably attributable to the support received. For reimbursable agreements, estimated costs must be reviewed annually to identify current-year actual costs. Its minimum-content table requires an agreement to address reimbursement for unavoidable termination expenses and annual review for financial impact.
- Retrieval note: The official PDF URL returned HTTP 403 to direct command-line retrieval on 2026-09-10, but the official issuance record and text were available through the Department of Defense Executive Services Directorate index result. The PDF is not committed.
- Boundary: This department-wide support-agreement instruction is not itself the IGSA-specific cost-savings certification required by 10 U.S.C. § 2679(e)(3), and its annual cost review does not prove that realized savings were verified.
