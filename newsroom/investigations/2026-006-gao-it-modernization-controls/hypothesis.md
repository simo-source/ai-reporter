# Hypothesis

GAO's public performance and budget records may not allow an outside reader to reconcile the progress of its IT-modernization initiatives with the total cost of the program after the agency moved from a five-year plan to an ongoing modernization strategy.

The OIG audit supplies the current control baseline: 39 of 61 initiatives were reported complete as of May 2026, while some smaller initiatives were tracked under larger projects. The earlier performance plan supplies a four-year enterprise-content-management roadmap and an 80% FY2024 target for an IT-tools measure against 56% actual performance in FY2022. The FY2025 performance report supplies a later resource-constraint disclosure, but these records do not yet join individual initiatives, costs, and outcomes.

This is a testable document-comparison hypothesis, not a finding about waste or failed delivery.
