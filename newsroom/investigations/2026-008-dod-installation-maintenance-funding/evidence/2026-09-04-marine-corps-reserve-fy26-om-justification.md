# E6 — Marine Corps Reserve FY2026 Operation and Maintenance justification

- URL: https://www.secnav.navy.mil/fmc/fmb/Documents/26pres/OMMCR_Book.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-09-04
- Filename: official Department of the Navy FY2026 Operation and Maintenance, Marine Corps Reserve justification book
- Locator: Appropriation Highlights, pages 5–6; Base Support, Subactivity Group BSM1, Exhibit OP-5, pages 35–36; “Performance Criteria and Evaluation Summary.”
- Captured excerpt: The book states that the Marine Corps Reserve FY2026 estimate funds facility sustainment at 53 percent of the OSD model. Its performance table lists a $41.846 million FY2026 sustainment requirement, $21.793 million sustainment funding, $0.489 million military-pay sustainment, and $22.282 million total sustainment funding; it reports “% Sustainment of FSM 26.2” as 53 percent. The same exhibit says the FSRM subactivity funds the Reserve headquarters and all reserve installations.
- Boundary: This is a Reserve-component budget request, not the active Marine Corps or the whole Department of Defense. The 53-percent figure is therefore a component-specific comparison point and cannot be used alone to contradict DOD’s department-wide 85-percent minimum/87-percent average framing.
