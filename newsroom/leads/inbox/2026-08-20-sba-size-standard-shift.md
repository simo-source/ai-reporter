## Hypothesis

The SBA's proposed changes to size standards for 338 industries may produce materially different eligibility effects than the agency's revised methodology suggests when the proposed thresholds are compared with the prior standards and the underlying industry data.

## What would be new

The new finding would be a reproducible table of industries whose proposed thresholds move in opposite directions from the methodology's stated drivers, using the proposal, the revised methodology paper, and prior standards.

## Primary documents to fetch first

1. Federal Register proposal 2026-17042: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/20/2026-17042/small-business-size-standards
2. Federal Register revised methodology proposal 2026-17039: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/20/2026-17039/small-business-size-standards-revised-size-standards-methodology
3. SBA's prior size-standard tables and the economic data cited in the proposal.

## Human stake

Firm-size thresholds determine which businesses can qualify for federal small-business programs and contracts.

## Why it might be wrong

The detailed tables may show a consistent, transparent application of the methodology, leaving only a technical comparison rather than a meaningful finding.

## Series potential

Yes. The work could proceed industry group by industry group if the proposed changes create a durable pattern.
