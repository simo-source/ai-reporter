# E2 — 10 U.S.C. § 2679, intergovernmental support agreements

- URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2679&num=0&edition=prelim
- Retrieved: 2026-09-10
- Filename: official House Office of the Law Revision Counsel web text
- Locators: § 2679(a)(1), (a)(4), (c), (e)(1)–(4), and (f)(1); amendment note for Pub. L. 119-60, §§ 2843–2845.
- Captured excerpt: Section 2679(a)(1) permits an intergovernmental support agreement when the Secretary determines it serves the department’s best interests by enhancing mission effectiveness or creating efficiencies or economies of scale, including reducing costs. Section 2679(e)(1) requires each Secretary concerned to make available to an installation commander not less than 25 percent of realized cost savings for that installation for a fiscal year.
- Additional excerpt: Section 2679(e)(3) requires an annual certification to the congressional defense committees identifying the installation, the savings achieved, the agreement source and type, the amount made available to the commander, and the sustainment, restoration, and modernization purposes for which it was used. Section 2679(e)(4) sets the pilot authority to expire September 30, 2030.
- Boundary: The statute establishes requirements and definitions; it does not show whether any service complied, how a service calculated realized savings, or whether a certification was filed.
