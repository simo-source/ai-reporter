# Hypothesis

## Testable proposition

NASA's own public accountability report shows that the agency had a large unresolved recommendation backlog and had formally declined to implement recommendations addressing Artemis mission cost visibility and spacecraft cybersecurity controls. GAO's current official record provides the narrower contemporaneous context: three priority recommendations identified in August 2025 remained unimplemented, and a fourth was identified in July 2026.

## What would falsify it

- NASA's report does not contain the 94-recommendation total or the two “Open, Will not Implement” entries.
- The GAO record is not an official primary record or does not state the current priority-recommendation status.
- The cited entries are not uniquely identifiable by report and recommendation number.

## Finding

The proposition is supported by the three archived official documents. The article must describe documented status and NASA's stated rationale, not infer intent, negligence, or operational harm.
