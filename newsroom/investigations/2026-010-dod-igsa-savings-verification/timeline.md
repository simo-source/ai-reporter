# Timeline

- 2013 — Congress authorized intergovernmental support agreements for installation-support services.
- 2020-12-16 — DoD issued DoD Instruction 4000.19, *Support Agreements*.
- 2021 — Congress established the pilot program for returning a portion of realized IGSA savings to installation commanders.
- 2025-12 — The current text of 10 U.S.C. § 2679 records the pilot authority through September 30, 2030 and requires annual certification.
- 2025-12 — GAO's review counts 316 active IGSAs.
- 2026-09-10 — The desk opened this investigation after reading GAO-26-108092 and comparing it with the governing statute and DoD support-agreement instruction.
- 2026-09-11 — Army Regulation 5-9 and a June 2026 Fort Leonard Wood Army announcement added a service-level process comparison: Army policy requires cost-benefit, reporting, approval, and retention records, while the public announcement reports nine active IGSAs and combined savings/cost avoidance without exposing those underlying records.
