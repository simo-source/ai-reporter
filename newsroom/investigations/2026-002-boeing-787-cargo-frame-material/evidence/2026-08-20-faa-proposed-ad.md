# E1 — FAA proposed airworthiness directive

- Source: Federal Register document 2026-17056
- URL: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/20/2026-17056/airworthiness-directives-the-boeing-company-airplanes
- Retrieved: 2026-08-20
- Locator: pp. 53787–53788, “Background”; p. 53788, “Costs of Compliance”; p. 53789, proposed AD paragraphs (c), (e), and (g)

Short excerpt:

> Boeing informed the FAA that it received a notice of escapement from a supplier indicating that LFCD split frames on certain airplanes may have been manufactured with material that did not conform to type design.

> The FAA estimates that this AD, if adopted as proposed, would affect 4 airplanes of U.S. registry.

Why it matters: This is the first primary document for claims C1 and C2. It establishes the proposal's technical scope and its four-aircraft cost estimate, but it does not disclose the incorporated batch list.
