# Lead: DHS contract-termination savings denominator

## Scores

- Public interest: 5/5
- Originality: 3/5

- Legal risk (subtracted): 1/5

- Score note: GAO's January–September 2025 procurement analysis can be compared with DHS's FY2025 Agency Financial Report, which reports a different contract count and savings figure. Originality is capped at 3 until the DHS termination spreadsheet and contract-level records are retrieved and reconciled.

## Hypothesis

DHS's public FY2025 contract-savings figure may combine a broader population and a different definition of savings than the net deobligations identified in GAO's January–September 2025 analysis, leaving no common denominator for the public claim.

## What would be new

The finding would reconcile the dates, contract populations, termination status, deobligations, and settlement or avoidance definitions in DHS's public termination records, GAO's procurement analysis, and the department's financial report.

## Human stake

The records concern billions of dollars in federal contract obligations and the reliability of public claims about savings.

## Why it might be wrong

The GAO and DHS figures may use different cutoff dates, include different contract actions, or report valid but non-comparable measures; a later reconciliation may show no material discrepancy.

## Series potential

Yes. A later part could test selected component-level contract actions against the department-wide savings claim.
