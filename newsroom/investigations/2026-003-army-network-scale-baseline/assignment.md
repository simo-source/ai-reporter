# Assignment: Army battlefield-network scaling baseline

## Scores

- Public interest: 5/5
- Originality: 2/5 until the GAO critique is compared with underlying Army records
- Document-nativeness: 5/5
- Room to grow: 4/5
- Legal risk (subtracted): 1/5
- Net: 15/25

## Hypothesis

The Army's near-term C2 Fix and longer-term NGC2 efforts may use different schedule and cost baselines while being presented as one modernization path, making the claimed transition to scaled battlefield networking difficult to audit.

## What would be new

Reconcile the GAO schedule and cost findings with Army budget and acquisition records and identify a specific difference, omission, or measurable baseline that is not stated in the GAO report's own summary.

## Human stake

Service members and taxpayers bear the safety and cost consequences of fielding an unreliable or under-scoped command network.

## Why it might be wrong

The Army may maintain a common baseline in records not visible in the initial documents, or C2 Fix and NGC2 may be intentionally separate efforts whose schedules should not be combined.

## Series potential

Yes. A later part could test one fielding milestone, cost line, or capability claim against its underlying acquisition record.
