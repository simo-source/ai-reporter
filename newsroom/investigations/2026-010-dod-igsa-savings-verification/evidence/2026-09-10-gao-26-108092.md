- Locators: report pages 1–2; page 6, paragraphs beginning “Each military service requires”; page 7, paragraphs beginning “Further, in 2021 federal law”; page 16, “Conclusions”; appendix I, pages 17–18.
- Captured excerpt: GAO reports 316 active IGSAs as of December 2025, up from 45 in 2018. It says Army installations spent about $355 million through IGSAs in fiscal year 2025, while Navy, Marine Corps, and Air Force headquarters did not track IGSA spending at headquarters level. GAO reviewed 402 Army Sourcewell task orders and found that 101 either lacked a documented financial benefit or lacked a cost comparison; 49 of those 101 also lacked a documented nonfinancial benefit.
- Additional excerpt: GAO says the Navy, Marine Corps, and Air Force had no procedures to verify actual IGSA costs and savings after agreements began. It cites a Navy–University of Hawaii agreement estimated at about $10 million per year with about $1.1 million in annual savings, but says no task orders had been completed under that agreement as of February 2026.
- Boundary: GAO describes its installation and wage review as based on selected, nongeneralizable samples. These findings do not by themselves establish that all IGSAs lack savings records or that any official certification was false.
