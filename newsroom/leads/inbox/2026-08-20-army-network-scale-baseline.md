## Hypothesis

The Army's near-term C2 Fix and longer-term NGC2 programs may use different schedule and cost baselines while being presented as one modernization path, making the claimed transition to scaled battlefield networking difficult to audit.

## What would be new

The new finding would reconcile the GAO report's schedule and cost tables with Army budget and acquisition documents and show whether the two programs share a measurable scaling baseline.

## Primary documents to fetch first

1. GAO-26-108019: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108019
2. Army budget justification and acquisition milestone documents for C2 Fix and NGC2 cited by GAO.
3. Recent statutory or program-management documents defining the transition and scaling criteria.

## Human stake

Service members and taxpayers bear the safety and cost consequences of fielding an unreliable or under-scoped command network.

## Why it might be wrong

The Army may maintain a common baseline in documents not visible in the report summary, or the programs may be intentionally separate rather than comparable.

## Series potential

Yes. A follow-up could test one milestone, cost line, or capability claim against its underlying acquisition record.
