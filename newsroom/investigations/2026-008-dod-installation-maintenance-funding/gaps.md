# Gaps

- The DOD overview's 85-percent minimum and 87-percent average are now accompanied by component-level request records: Army regular at 91 percent, Navy at 85 percent, and Marine Corps Reserve at 53 percent. The records still do not identify whether the department-wide percentages use a weighted population, include reserve components, or use the same treatment of military-pay and reconciliation funds.
- No service-level record yet joins facility-sustainment requirements, budget requests, obligations, work orders, and backlog or condition changes for the same population and period. The new service books provide modeled requirements and requests, but not an outcome series.
- The Comptroller's FY2025 DD 1416 archive confirms that Army and Navy Title II O&M execution workbooks exist, but the workbook links were not exposed in the accessible page view. The Army or Navy workbook still must be retrieved and read before any execution claim is made.
- USACE's public Civil Works work-order standard identifies useful status, cost, date, and closeout fields, but it is not an Army regular-component FSRM execution record and cannot close the denominator gap.
- GAO's eight-installation sample is purposeful and non-generalizable; site-level observations cannot be extended to all DOD facilities.
- The reported $285 billion deferred-maintenance figure is a DOD-reported estimate cited by GAO, not a measurement of work completed or a direct finding of harm.

## Decision required

Continue while an official DD 1416 execution workbook or service-level obligation, facility-condition, or work-order record can pair with the service request denominators. Park after two days with the same direct-workbook retrieval action or three consecutive continue decisions without a new primary excerpt. Kill if only budget percentages and generic workflow policy can be compared without an outcome measure, or if service records cannot identify a comparable population and period.
