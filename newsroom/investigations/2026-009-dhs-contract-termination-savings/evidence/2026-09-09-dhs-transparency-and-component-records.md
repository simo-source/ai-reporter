- Locators: transparency page body and “List of Terminated Contracts (XLSX)” button; each component page’s attachment table.
- Captured excerpts: DHS says the button downloads a spreadsheet of all contract actions and directs readers to contracting-activity files. The component pages list individual contract attachments and their publication dates. Examples include CBP’s 70B01C21F00001502 attachment dated 03/10/2025, CISA’s 70RCSA21FR0000079 attachment dated 03/06/2025, and USSS’s 70US0923P70092346 attachment dated 03/11/2025.
- Retrieval note: the button’s official target, `https://www.dhs.gov/xlibrary/terminated-contracts/dhs-transparent-contract-terminations.xlsx?-01xlsx_X01`, returned HTTP 403. The target without the query string, with `download=1`, at an alternate DHS path, and over HTTP also returned 403 the same day.
- Why it matters: Establishes that DHS publishes a spreadsheet and component-level records, but the inaccessible spreadsheet remains the missing population-level record for reconciling counts, dates, and savings.
