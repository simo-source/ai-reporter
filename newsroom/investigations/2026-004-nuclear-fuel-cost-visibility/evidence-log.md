## E1 — GAO-26-107385, Nuclear Fuel

- URL: https://files.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-107385.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-08-25
- Locator: PDF p. 1; pp. 40–42; pp. 51–52.
- Captured excerpt: GAO says DOE had awarded $2.7 billion in task orders but had not documented an economic analysis of how its support could affect commercial LEU and HALEU production.
