# E2 — Department of the Navy FY2026 O&M budget

- URL: https://www.secnav.navy.mil/fmc/fmb/Documents/26pres/OMN_Book.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-08-30
- Locator: “PBA-19 Appropriation Highlights,” “Ship Maintenance,” and “Budget Activity 2: Mobilization.”
- Captured excerpt: The budget describes funding for 97% of total ship-maintenance requirements and 57 CNO availabilities, projects 36,488 naval-shipyard FTEs, and supports 27 private-sector CNO availabilities. Its mobilization section gives a $1.779 billion FY2026 estimate and a plan to inactivate 14 ships, five before end of service life.
- Why it matters: This pre-existing Navy budget record supplies the FY2026 resource and inactivation-response baseline; it does not report the class-level SSN measure in E1.
