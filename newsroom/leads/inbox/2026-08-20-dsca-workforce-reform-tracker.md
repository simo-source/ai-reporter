## Hypothesis

The Defense Security Cooperation Agency's count of completed workforce-reform provisions may conceal uneven implementation across the five teams and a dependency on work scheduled for fiscal year 2028.

## What would be new

The new finding would map each provision's statutory requirement to DSCA implementation evidence and expose any mismatch between completion labels and the operational deliverables described in agency records.

## Primary documents to fetch first

1. GAO-26-107976: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-107976
2. The FY2024 NDAA workforce provisions and DSCA implementation documents cited by GAO.
3. DSCA workforce plans, milestones, or oversight records showing the status of the 66 provisions.

## Human stake

Security-cooperation staffing affects how the United States administers defense relationships and assistance.

## Why it might be wrong

The report may already provide a complete provision-level status table, leaving no independent mismatch to investigate.

## Series potential

Yes. A series could examine implementation evidence by reform area without repeating the GAO summary.
