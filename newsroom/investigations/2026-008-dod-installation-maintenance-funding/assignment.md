# Assignment

## Hypothesis

DOD's 90-percent installation-maintenance funding goal may not be a reliable proxy for reducing the facilities backlog because service budget requests, funded requirements, and executed work may measure different populations or time periods.

## What would be new

The finding would reconcile GAO's five-year funding and backlog measures with service budget and execution records, showing whether the FY2026 funding framing tracks actual backlog reduction or leaves a measurable gap.

## Human stake

Installation conditions affect service-member safety, mission readiness, and billions of dollars in public maintenance obligations.

## Why it might be wrong

The apparent gap may result from different accounting periods or facility populations rather than a mismatch in DOD's funding goal, and service records may show that funded work is reducing the backlog.

## Series potential

Yes. A later part could compare one military service's funding request, execution, and facility-condition measures against GAO's cross-service baseline.
