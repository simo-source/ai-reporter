# E7 — Congressional Budget Office, Navy shipbuilding testimony

- URL: https://www.congress.gov/119/meeting/house/119209/documents/HHRG-119-AS28-20260422-SD010.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-08-31
- Locator: April 22, 2026 testimony, PDF p. 10, “Effects of a Larger Fleet on Ship Maintenance.”
- Captured excerpt: CBO says the Navy’s goal is to have no more than 20% of SSNs in or awaiting maintenance; “currently” 33% of the SSN fleet is undergoing or awaiting maintenance, the largest share in any year since 2008 other than 2021. It says a 48-boat force with one-third in maintenance could at best deploy 32 SSNs.
- Why it matters: This is a later, independent congressional primary with the same maintenance-or-awaiting-maintenance measure as CRS E1. It places the reported share at 33% after E1’s FY2024 34%, but does not provide a Navy execution date or prove that the count fell.
