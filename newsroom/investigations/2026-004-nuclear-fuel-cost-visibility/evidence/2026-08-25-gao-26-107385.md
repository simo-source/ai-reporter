- Locator: PDF p. 1, “What GAO Found”; pp. 40–42, section “DOE Has Not Conducted an Economic Analysis of How Its Efforts to Support the Expansion of Enrichment Capacity Could Impact the Enriched Uranium Supply Market”; pp. 51–52, “Recommendations.”
- Captured excerpt: GAO reports that DOE awarded $2.7 billion in task orders for HALEU and LEU capacity, but “did not document any analysis” showing how the support would affect new commercial production or whether the actions were appropriately sized. GAO also says DOE had not finalized a strategic plan, life-cycle cost estimate, preliminary master schedule, or its required HALEU report to Congress as of July 2026.
- Why it matters: Establishes the baseline claim that major awards preceded documented economic and program-management controls. It is not, by itself, proof that the awards were mis-sized or ineffective.
