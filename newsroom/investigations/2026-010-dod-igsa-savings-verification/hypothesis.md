# Hypothesis

DOD's rapid expansion of intergovernmental support agreements may have outpaced the records needed to verify realized savings and calculate the amounts that the statute requires the services to return to installations for sustainment, restoration, and modernization.

The first primary comparison is not a finding. GAO reports 316 active IGSAs as of December 2025, describes missing or incomplete cost comparisons in selected multi-installation and multi-service agreements, and says the Navy, Marine Corps, and Air Force lack procedures to verify actual costs and savings. Section 2679 of Title 10 separately requires each Secretary concerned to make at least 25 percent of realized installation-level savings available for sustainment, restoration, and modernization, and requires annual certification to Congress.

This investigation will test whether public records connect agreement-level cost estimates, actual costs, realized savings, the amount returned to installations, and the required sustainment purposes. It will not infer noncompliance from GAO's sample or treat an estimated saving as a realized one.
