# E3 — GAO-26-109256, Navy Readiness

- URL: https://files.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-109256.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-08-30
- Locator: PDF pp. 8–13.
- Captured excerpt: GAO reports 18,687 lost operational days and an estimated $4.17 billion in operating and support costs for fiscal years 2016–2025, and projects more than 14,000 inactive-idle days and $3.1 billion in costs for fiscal years 2026–2030. It also says the Navy implemented the 2018 recommendations but did not eliminate the maintenance backlog.
- Why it matters: This is the investigation's operating-day and inactive-idle baseline; it must remain analytically separate from E1's annual class-level maintenance-or-idle count.
