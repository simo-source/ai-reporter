# Hypothesis

The FAA's proposed inspection for certain Boeing 787-8 large forward cargo-door split frames may cover a broader type-and-batch population than the four U.S.-registered airplanes used in the agency's public cost estimate.

The first comparison is now supported as a scope distinction: the incorporated bulletin lists nine 787-8 line numbers in its effectivity, while the proposal's cost section says its estimate affects four airplanes of U.S. registry. FAA registry records confirm four valid U.S.-registered 787-8s whose serial numbers match the candidate four-airframe crosswalk, but the public FAA records do not contain Boeing line numbers. The reconciliation therefore remains a provisional inference rather than a publishable primary-document finding; a primary Boeing or FAA crosswalk is still required.
