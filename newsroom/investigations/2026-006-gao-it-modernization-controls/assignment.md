## Hypothesis

GAO's public performance and budget records may not allow an outside reader to reconcile the progress of its IT-modernization initiatives with the total cost of the program after the agency moved from a five-year plan to an ongoing strategy.

## What would be new

The finding would compare the OIG's initiative count and cost-tracking limitation with GAO's earlier roadmap, performance target, and later resource-deferral disclosures, testing whether the public record exposes a measurable cost-and-progress trail without treating the audit summary as proof of waste.
