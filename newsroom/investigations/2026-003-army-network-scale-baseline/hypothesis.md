# Hypothesis

The Army's near-term C2 Fix and longer-term NGC2 efforts may use different schedule and cost baselines while being presented as one modernization path, making the claimed transition to scaled battlefield networking difficult to audit.

Initial testing shows that the Army's FY2026 public budget material describes NGC2 as a four-layer investment and promises fielding to two divisions and a corps, while the detailed RDT&E justification describes prototype maturation, evaluation, and later transition to programs of record. The relationship between those budget promises, the 2025 prototype award, and the service-wide fielding goal remains unproved.
