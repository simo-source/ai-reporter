# E1 — GAO-26-107255

- URL: https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-107255/index.html
- Retrieved: 2026-09-03
- Filename: official GAO accessible report text
- Locator: Highlights, “What GAO Found”; Letter, “DOD Has Not Requested Full Funding for Maintenance Activities,” paragraphs 134–160.

GAO reports that DOD's deferred maintenance increased from $137 billion in FY2020 to an estimated $285 billion in FY2025. It says the 2014 policy goal was to fund regular maintenance at 90 percent or higher of Facilities Sustainment Model recommendations; for FY2021–FY2025, the Army, Navy, and Air Force generally requested around 80 percent, while Marine Corps requests fell from 80 percent to 40 percent. GAO also reports that none of the eight contacted installations received funding meeting the 90-percent goal.

Boundary: GAO says the eight-installation sample was purposeful and non-generalizable; the site figures are anecdotal evidence, not a department-wide estimate.
