- Locator: “Department of Energy Oversight Policy,” opening policy description.
- Captured excerpt: DOE says DOE Policy 226.2 establishes expectations for federal oversight and contractor assurance systems, while DOE Order 226.1B requires contractors to implement effective CASs that provide assurance that work is conducted safely and securely, risks are managed, and internal controls function efficiently. DOE says CAS information supports a risk-based, tailored approach to oversight across safety, security, cybersecurity, environment, quality, and business and finance.
